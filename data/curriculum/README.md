# Curriculum data policy

The MVP currently contains original, short learning prompts aligned to broad mathematics concepts. It does not bundle official textbook pages or reproduce Ministry assessment materials.

Official discovery portal: https://studentbooks.moe.gov.eg/

For every future curriculum item, record:
- academic year (e.g. 2026/2027)
- grade, subject, term, unit and lesson
- official source URL and retrieval date
- whether content is an original summary, public metadata, or licensed excerpt
- rights/permission status

Do not scrape or redistribute complete Ministry textbooks. The Ministry's published curriculum notice states that intellectual property rights in the developed curricula belong to the Egyptian state represented by the Ministry. Obtain legal permission before storing or distributing protected textbook text, images, or PDFs.
